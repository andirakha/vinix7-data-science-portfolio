## Deck: "From Insight to Action - Data-Driven Marketing Strategy"
**Source analysis:** [Module 5](../module-05-data-visualization/) - static visualization with Python.
**Data used:** shopping behavior (annual income, spending score 0-100) and demographics (age, gender).

### Background and goals
- **Problem:** Marketing is currently mass-market ("one size fits all"), risking wasted budget on ineffective approaches such as gender targeting.
- **Need:** A deeper understanding of who the customers are and how they shop.
- **Goals:** identify customer segments by income vs spending; understand the main demographics (age); validate the assumption that gender affects spending; provide the basis for a move to personalized, segment-based marketing.

### Key findings
1. **Five customer segments** with distinct shopping characteristics, so marketing should be tailored to each group.
2. **Most customers are of working age (20-40)**, making them the primary target for digital campaigns and more relevant brand communication.
3. **Gender is not significant:** shopping behavior is not driven by gender, so gender-based campaigns are less effective.

### Recommendations
1. **Adopt segment-based marketing:** loyalty programs for high-income customers and targeted promotions for low-income segments.
2. **Optimize branding for working-age customers:** use social media such as Instagram and TikTok and influencer collaborations for ages 20-40.
3. **Shift budget from gender campaigns to income-based segmentation:** target the factors that actually drive behavior - income and spending patterns.

### Call to action
| Stop | Focus | Start |
|---|---|---|
| Funding gender-specific campaigns (not proven effective) | Channels: Instagram, TikTok, influencers | Loyalty program for the "Ideal Target" segment; discount / installment promotions for the "Low-Income Spenders" segment |

## Poster: "3 Key Insights to Maximize Marketing ROI"
*Subtitle: turning data into smarter marketing strategy.* The poster reuses the charts from Module 5, one per insight:

| Insight | Stop | Start |
|---|---|---|
| **1. We have 5 customer types, not 1** | Applying an ineffective "one size fits all" marketing strategy | Applying segmented marketing |
| **2. Our core market is working age (20-40)** | Wasting resources on campaigns that target less relevant demographics | Focusing branding and ad channels on the platforms where young adults are most active |
| **3. Gender is not a significant differentiator in shopping** | Allocating significant budget to campaigns focused exclusively on gender | Shifting budget and resources to the factors that actually matter |

**Key conclusion and recommendation:** stop "one size fits all" marketing and move to a strategy focused on **behavior segments** and **working-age customers** to maximize impact, budget efficiency, and marketing return on investment (ROI).
