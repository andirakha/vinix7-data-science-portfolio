# Module 1 - Data Diary: One Day as a Data Scientist

## Assignment context
The first module introduces data science: building data sense and intuition, storing data properly, fundamentals of AI algorithms, and storytelling with data. The assignment was to "dissect the YouTube algorithm" by analyzing one's own watch history with a four-step framework: data collection, facts, insight, and strategic action.

## Deliverable
A short write-up based on a personal YouTube watch history (the supporting screenshots are intentionally not included in this repository).

## 1. Data collection
The watch history shows a strong preference for simulation, strategy, and survival games, alongside interest in social and political issues, technology, science, and music. Content formats range from gameplay and tutorials to mods, in-depth analysis, and reviews.

## 2. Facts
- Strong interest in games that emphasize creativity, strategy, exploration, and survival.
- The viewer actively looks for ways to improve their own play experience.
- Beyond gaming, there is broad curiosity across social issues, politics, and science.

## 3. Insight
The main motivation for using YouTube is not passive entertainment but learning and taking on challenges. The viewer also shows a tendency to understand how things work, whatever the domain.

## 4. Strategic actions (from the platform's perspective)
- Recommend content that connects different user interests.
- Prioritize videos with in-depth analysis.
- Surface channels focused on challenge runs, speedruns, or advanced modding guides more often.
- Build personalized playlists that combine several interests.

## Takeaway
Viewing habits reflect thinking style and interest patterns:
- The viewer looks for learning, not only entertainment.
- Curiosity spans many fields.
- There is a consistent pattern of interest in complex systems.
- YouTube serves as an important learning medium.
