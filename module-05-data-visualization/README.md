# Module 5 - Data Visualization & Insight Communication

## Assignment context
After finding (Module 2), cleaning (Module 3), and exploring (Module 4) the data, the participant becomes the "data spokesperson": turning insights into effective, persuasive visuals. This repository holds **Option A (fundamental): communicate insights through static visualizations**. Pick 2-3 insights from Module 4, recreate each chart applying good visualization principles, and explain what decision a stakeholder would make from it.

## Files
| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `data_visualization_insight_communication.ipynb` | K-Means segmentation (k = 5), gender comparison, and age distribution charts |

**Data:** reads `data/processed/mall_customers_clean.csv`, generated by [`module-03-data-cleaning/data_cleaning.ipynb`](../module-03-data-cleaning/data_cleaning.ipynb) (a copy is included). The notebook is committed with its outputs.

## Insight 1 - Five customer segments based on shopping behavior
*Chart:* scatter plot of annual income vs spending score, colored by K-Means segment.

*Stakeholder decision (Marketing Manager):* shift from mass marketing to personalized, segment-based marketing and allocate budget more efficiently, e.g., an exclusive loyalty program for the "Ideal Target" segment (high income, high spending) and discount or installment campaigns for low-income free spenders to lift conversion.

## Insight 2 - Most customers are of working age
*Chart:* age histogram with the 20-40 range highlighted.

*Stakeholder decision (Brand Manager):* focus branding and communication on ages 20-40, use channels popular with young adults (e.g., Instagram, TikTok), adapt tone of voice, and partner with influencers whose audiences are in this range, so resources reach the largest and most promising market.

## Insight 3 - Gender is not a significant factor in shopping behavior
*Chart:* average spending score by gender (nearly identical bars).

*Stakeholder decision (Head of Strategy):* do not fund gender-specific campaigns. Redirect effort to factors that matter more, such as the income / spending segments from Insight 1, to maximize ROI.

These three insights are repackaged into a presentation in [Module 10](../module-10-data-storytelling/).
